## Thesis Summary

**Hut 8 is a former Bitcoin miner that has repositioned itself as a 'power-first' energy-infrastructure and AI data center developer — effectively an AI real estate / landlord model.** Its core skill (originating and securing large-scale power, then building purpose-built campuses) is being redeployed from mining into long-duration, triple-net, take-or-pay leases with investment-grade AI tenants. As of Q2 2026 it had 949 MW of contracted AI capacity across two campuses (River Bend, LA and Beacon Point, TX), ~$26.6B of expected base-term contract value, >$1.75B of expected average annual NOI, and $7.5B of non-recourse, non-dilutive project financing secured. It spun its mining operations into separately-listed American Bitcoin (ABTC), in which it retains a majority stake, and holds a sizeable Bitcoin treasury.

**The central tension:** the contracted lease book is enormous relative to today's tiny revenue ($74.9M in Q2) and the lease cash flows don't begin until ~Q2 2027 — so the stock is a high-beta bet on flawless multi-gigawatt construction execution, tenant/counterparty credit, and Bitcoin, wrapped in a complex holding-company structure.

- **Q2 2026 (reported Aug 4, 2026):** Revenue $74.9M (+81% YoY), compute revenue $72.5M (>2x), 935 BTC mined, gross margin ~64% (from 47%), adjusted EBITDA $10.4M. But a $138M non-cash digital-asset (Bitcoin) loss drove a GAAP net loss of $177.1M.

- **Structural complexity:** Parent (HUT) = energy/AI infra developer; majority-owned, separately-listed American Bitcoin (ABTC) = the mining/BTC-treasury vehicle (partly backed by Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr.). Value must be assessed sum-of-the-parts.

## 1. Company Information

### Business Overview

Hut 8 Corp. (Nasdaq/TSX: HUT), historically headquartered in Toronto with U.S. operations, was one of North America's largest and oldest Bitcoin miners (founded 2017). It has repositioned as an energy-infrastructure platform that develops and operates data centers for AI, high-performance computing (HPC), and ASIC compute. Management now describes the business across segments such as Power (originating/developing/managing electricity capacity), Digital Infrastructure (data center campuses), and Compute (ASIC/mining hosting and HPC), and explicitly frames Hut 8 as 'an energy infrastructure platform, not a Bitcoin miner.'

The model is deliberately analogous to a data center REIT / landlord: Hut 8 originates power at scale, secures long-lead sites that incumbents overlooked, builds NVIDIA-spec campuses (liquid cooling at >140 kW/rack via Vertiv, EPCM by Jacobs), and commercializes them with 15-year triple-net, take-or-pay leases to investment-grade tenants — then finances construction with non-recourse project bonds that amortize through the lease payments, returning equity to the parent without dilution. This sits at the same 'grid-to-chip' layer as Vertiv and Forgent, but Hut 8 owns the campus and the power interconnect and collects the rent, rather than selling equipment into it.

**The two-entity structure:** In 2025 Hut 8 carved its Bitcoin mining business into American Bitcoin Corp. (ABTC), a separately-listed company (reverse-merged with Gryphon Digital) that Hut 8 majority-owns and that is partly backed by Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr. ABTC 'doubled down' on mining and a Bitcoin treasury (and its shares fell sharply in 2026), while the Hut 8 parent pivoted to AI infrastructure. Hut 8 also retains its own Bitcoin treasury (~16k BTC at mid-2026 per some sources) as a 'bridge' asset until lease revenue begins.

### Leadership

**CEO: Asher Genoot —** Architect of the power-first pivot; frames electricity as 'one of the scarcest resources in the economy' and the company as building a repeatable origination-to-lease-to-project-finance flywheel. Also associated with the ABTC vehicle. His credibility is now tied to converting an 8+ GW pipeline into contracted, financed campuses on schedule.

**CFO: Sean Glennon —** Emphasizes the reconciliation between large GAAP net losses (driven by Bitcoin mark-to-market) and a growing operating business and adjusted EBITDA; steward of the non-recourse, investment-grade financing strategy with an investment-grade corporate rating as the stated 'north star.'

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)

- **AI data center capacity / power:** The binding constraint on AI is increasingly power and speed-to-energization; Hut 8 competes to originate and lease gigawatts of contracted capacity to hyperscalers and neoclouds.
- **Contracted book already vast:** ~$26.6B base-term contract value and >$1.75B expected average annual NOI from just 949 MW — before the rest of the pipeline converts.
- **Development pipeline:** Management has cited a multi-gigawatt pipeline (reported at various points from ~8.4 GW to ~9 GW across exclusivity, development, construction, management, and diligence), with potential to expand Energy Capacity Under Management materially if commercialized.

### Market Position & Competition

Hut 8 competes both with other repositioned crypto miners and with pure-play AI data center developers/neoclouds for power, sites, and tenants. Its differentiation is speed-to-power on overlooked sites plus a proven ability to attach investment-grade project financing to a single-sponsor campus.

| Company / Group | Position / Notes |

| Miner-to-AI pivots (IREN, TeraWulf, Cipher, Core Scientific, Riot, MARA) | Peer set also converting power/sites to AI/HPC leasing; competing for tenants and financing. Execution and tenant credit differentiate winners. |
| Neoclouds / AI developers (CoreWeave, Crusoe, Fluidstack, Vantage, Aligned) | Direct competitors for hyperscaler capacity; Fluidstack is also a Hut 8 tenant/operator partner (River Bend). |

**Hut 8's competitive advantages:** (1) A repeatable, proven power-first origination model (River Bend then Beacon Point on identical triple-net, take-or-pay terms); (2) first-mover access to the investment-grade construction bond market for single-sponsor data centers — non-recourse, non-dilutive financing at ~95% loan-to-cost that returns equity to the parent; (3) blue-chip ecosystem (Google guarantee at River Bend, Anthropic as a partner/tenant, Vertiv, Jacobs, JPM, Goldman) validating counterparty quality; (4) large multi-GW pipeline and existing power/site assets from the mining era. The mirror-image risks: execution at multi-GW scale is unproven at completion, several tenants are undisclosed, and the model depends on continued cheap non-recourse financing and hyperscaler demand.

## 4. Investment Thesis: Bull vs. Bear

### Bull Case

**A repeatable 'AI landlord' flywheel with a $26.6B contracted book and non-dilutive financing.** Hut 8 has converted its hardest-won mining-era asset — the ability to source and secure power at scale — into long-duration, investment-grade, triple-net lease cash flows. River Bend proved the model; Beacon Point proved it is repeatable across tenants and geographies without a Google backstop. Because construction is funded with non-recourse project bonds that amortize through the leases, the parent grows NOI without diluting shareholders and even recycles equity out of each project.

- **Enormous contracted visibility:** 949 MW, ~$26.6B base-term value, >$1.75B expected average annual NOI, 15-year terms, investment-grade counterparties.
- **Non-dilutive, non-recourse financing:** $7.5B secured; ~95% loan-to-cost; first single-sponsor DC in the IG construction-bond market; returns equity to parent.
- **Repeatability + pipeline:** ~8–9 GW pipeline; Beacon Point Phase 2 and River Bend expansion are near-term conversion catalysts toward >2.5 GW under management.
- **Blue-chip validation:** Google guarantee, Anthropic partnership, Vertiv, Jacobs, JPM, Goldman — a credible ecosystem for a former miner.
- **Optionality:** ABTC majority stake + ~16k BTC treasury provide a bridge and upside if Bitcoin recovers.
- **Operating leverage already visible:** ~64% gross margin and rising adjusted EBITDA before any AI-lease revenue.

### Bear Case

**A pre-revenue construction story valued at ~$10.5B, wrapped in Bitcoin volatility and structural complexity.** Almost none of the ~$26.6B contract value is in the P&L yet — lease revenue doesn't begin until ~Q2 2027 — so the equity is a leveraged bet on delivering multiple gigawatts of NVIDIA-spec data centers on time and on budget, on tenant credit (several undisclosed), and on continued cheap non-recourse financing. Meanwhile GAAP results swing on Bitcoin, and value must be triangulated through a majority-owned, separately-listed mining affiliate.

- **Execution risk at scale:** Multi-GW construction (liquid cooling >140 kW/rack) is unproven through completion/energization; delays hit the whole thesis.
- **Timing gap:** Revenue ramps ~Q2 2027; the market is paying today for cash flows years out.
- **Counterparty/tenant opacity:** Several tenants are undisclosed 'investment-grade'; concentration in a few large leases; take-or-pay only as good as the credit behind it.
- **Bitcoin dependence:** $138M non-cash BTC loss produced a $177M GAAP loss; HUT treasury + ABTC stake inject large BTC-price volatility. ABTC shares fell ~76% in 2026.
- **Structural complexity / related party:** ABTC is both affiliate and customer; SOTP valuation and governance are harder to underwrite. Political backing (Trump family) adds headline risk.
- **Financing-market dependence:** Model relies on continued access to cheap IG non-recourse project debt; a credit-market shift raises cost of capital.
- **Extreme volatility:** Beta ~3.3; ~$9–$88 52-week range; regulatory/grid-cost scrutiny (Texas, PJM) is a live overhang.

### Balanced Read

Hut 8 is the most business-model-transformed name in this AI-infrastructure series and the hardest to value. Unlike Vertiv (sells the gear on a secure backlog), Micron (cycle-timing debate), Marvell (customer-retention debate), or Forgent (governance/structure on a real order book), Hut 8's question is whether a former miner can execute as a gigawatt-scale AI landlord and whether the reported financials — dominated by Bitcoin marks and construction accounting — can be seen through to the underlying contracted NOI. The contracted book is genuinely large and the non-dilutive financing model is elegant and, so far, repeatable. But the equity is pre-cash-flow on its core thesis, exposed to Bitcoin on two fronts, and structurally complex. This is a high-conviction-or-avoid name: it rewards investors who believe in the execution and can stomach beta ~3.3, and punishes those who need current earnings or clean structure.

## 5. Summary & Verdict

**Hut 8 has executed one of the more striking pivots in the AI-infrastructure landscape: from Bitcoin miner to power-first AI data center landlord with a ~$26.6B contracted lease book and a non-recourse, non-dilutive financing model that so far looks repeatable.** River Bend and Beacon Point together represent 949 MW of contracted, investment-grade, 15-year triple-net capacity and >$1.75B of expected average annual NOI, financed with $7.5B of project bonds that amortize through the leases. If the campuses energize on schedule and the ~8–9 GW pipeline converts, the earnings power is a step-change from today's $74.9M quarterly revenue.

**The caution is that almost all of that value is in the future, and the path there is unusually complex.** Core AI-lease revenue does not begin until ~Q2 2027, so the equity is a leveraged, high-beta (~3.3) bet on multi-gigawatt construction execution, tenant credit (several undisclosed), continued cheap financing, and Bitcoin — which drove a $177M GAAP loss this quarter and whose exposure runs through both HUT's own treasury and its majority-owned, separately-listed ABTC affiliate. This is a name to value sum-of-the-parts and to size for volatility, not on headline GAAP earnings.

### Position Sizing Considerations

- Treat as a high-volatility, high-conviction-or-avoid position (beta ~3.3; ~$9–$88 52-week range); size small.
- Correlated with the same AI-capex macro bet as Vertiv, Micron, Marvell, and Forgent — and adds two extra risk factors those don't have: Bitcoin price and holding-company structure.
- Notable ecosystem overlap with Vertiv (Hut 8's thermal/power partner) — a HUT position is partly a second-order VRT bet, compounding existing concentration.
- If underwriting HUT, do it on a sum-of-the-parts basis and wait for the ~Q2 2027 revenue-commencement proof point before sizing up; the ~Nov 18 print is the next checkpoint.

### Key Monitoring Points

- Construction/energization milestones at River Bend and Beacon Point (on-time delivery is the whole thesis).
- Lease-revenue commencement (~Q2 2027) and first contracted NOI hitting the P&L.
- Beacon Point Phase 2 (~500 MW) exercise and River Bend expansion (~330 MW) power decision.
- New lease signings and disclosure of tenant identities/credit quality.
- Progress toward an investment-grade corporate rating and continued access to non-recourse project debt.
- Bitcoin price, the size of HUT's treasury, and the value/performance of the ABTC stake.
- Regulatory/grid-cost scrutiny (Texas directives, PJM electricity-cost debates).
